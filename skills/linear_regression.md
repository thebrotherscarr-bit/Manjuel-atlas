# Skill: Linear Regression Forecasting
- **Action Keyword:** linear_regression
- **Description:** Fits a trendline through a series of numbers using ordinary least squares and projects the next few steps. Reports the slope, intercept and R-squared, and says plainly when the points do not actually lie on a line. Use it for historical figures - revenue, counts, timings - where a direction or a rough projection is wanted.
- **Parameters Needed:** <content>The numbers to fit, in order. Any separator; text around them is ignored.</content>
