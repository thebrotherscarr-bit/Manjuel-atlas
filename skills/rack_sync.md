# Skill: Sync the Rack
- **Action Keyword:** rack_sync
- **Says:** rack rebuild, rebuild the rack, resync the rack, sync the rack, rack sync
- **Description:** Pulls a current list of models from Ollama and rewrites rack.md - what is installed, what is loaded, what this ground declares, what is declared but missing, and what is installed but unused. Use after pulling or removing a model, or when the written rack looks stale. Reads Ollama fresh rather than a cached list.
- **Parameters Needed:** None
