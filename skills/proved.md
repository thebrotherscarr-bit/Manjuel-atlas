# Skill: What Has Been Proved

- **Action Keyword:** proved
- **Description:** Reports what the suites last proved and when — the stroke and smoke tallies, whether they are STALE against the current code, any failures with their addresses, the last few runs from the history, and whether the capability manifest agrees with the disk. Read from files the suites and the reconciler stamp themselves. Use for "have you run the tests", "what have you proved", "are the suites green", "what are your limitations", "is the ground stale".
- **Parameters Needed:** None
- **Says:** have you run the tests, have you run a full test suite, what have you proved, are the suites green, what did the suites prove, is the ground stale, what are your limitations, what can you not do
