## What these are

THE ESTATE LAWS (`law/ESTATE_LAWS.md`) bind the seats. These bind the HANDS — any agent,
human or model, working in `Desktop\Research` during a sitting. Each was
spoken by the operator after a sitting in which its absence cost him
something, and each is written here in the record's own words. The seven
standing rules in `CLAUDE.md` remain in force beside these; a SITTING LAW
is a rule that has been earned twice.

Cited as **SITTING LAW n**. Never as a bare `LAW n` — that name belongs to
the estate laws.

## THE SITTING LAWS

1. **Never assume anything about a file not read in full. Lies are not
   tolerated.** A hand that has grepped a file has not read it. A hand
   that has not read it may not assert what it says, what it lacks, or
   whether a thing was done. Earned 2026-09-03 (DAYBOOK session 2): an
   agent grepped `parity.py`, told the operator the parity had never run
   while `parity_history.jsonl` held the run, and left a task open that
   he had already closed. Both were on disk.

2. **Client material is never opened, named, or referred to unless the
   operator points at it in that message, for that act.** Not to answer a
   question, not to be thorough. If it seems relevant, ask first and wait.
   Earned 2026-09-02 (DAYBOOK session 1): two strokes had been reading a
   client folder on every run; every such reference was removed from the
   code, the tests, the docs and the log.

3. **Always start small.** Pick the smallest model that could do the job
   and move up only when its transcripts show it failing at its own job —
   never in anticipation of failure. Earned 2026-08-29 (memory.md,
   OPERATOR): every seat had been moved to 4b on a reasoned ceiling, not a
   measured floor. Recorded again 2026-09-03 when the rack was found to
   have moved past the ruling with no measurement written down.

4. **Research stays clean and organized. No folder is created and nothing
   is nested without asking. Ever.** A hand that needs a place to put
   something asks where; it does not invent one. Earned 2026-09-04
   (DAYBOOK session 4): an agent put two new law files into
   a nested `law/Archive/law/` and named them by a pattern it chose, without asking
   where the laws should live or what they should be called. The nesting
   itself was inherited from an older system's `core\Archive\law` and
   never questioned; the law is that a hand questions it, out loud,
   before adding to it. The folder was flattened to `law/` the same day.
