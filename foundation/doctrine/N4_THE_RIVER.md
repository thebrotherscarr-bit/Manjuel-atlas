# N4 — THE RIVER
### The law of the living estate: the garden, the chancery, and the current

Stands beside N1 (the Estate), N2 (its Constitution), N3 (the Provision), and
F2 (the Forge Road). Where those name what the estate IS, this names how it
MOVES — how the house runs itself without a hand on every gate, and where every
one of its motions stops and waits for the operator's word.

---

## I. The three organs of motion

**THE GARDEN** (`neiro/garden.py`) — the living population. The old estate kept
a garden in the Forge's sand: mortal figures that moved, reported, and poofed.
This is that garden grown lawful. What is planted now is worker stewards —
SPROUTS — each holding a real Steward body (constructing one wakes no heart),
each bound by the worker's charter:

> Workers observe and report. Only the prime Steward's hands move, and only
> behind the operator's gate. A sprout never writes a file of the estate's,
> never wakes a heart of its own, never inherits the hands.

The garden opens as a COUNCIL — three sprouts, sequenced, one working at a
time, every move legible. The cap is the operator's to raise once the ground
has proven the shape (the army of 300 is a name, not a number; it is earned
bed by bed). An idle sprout is not deleted; it is FOLDED to the season's
strata, its record intact — the growth law holds in the garden too. The garden
keeps no ledger of its own: the roster is derived, and every planting and
withering is filed on the public record by the chancery.

**THE CHANCERY** (`neiro/chancery.py`) — the records office. The clerk that
puts the estate's work into words: a due rite becomes a WRIT on the commons; a
worker's finished reading becomes a RETURN filed against that writ; a beat of
the river becomes ONE witness line carried to Manjuel through the heart's only
door, where HE weighs it and HE records it. Three laws, held structurally:

1. The chancery OWNS NO RECORD — it writes only to the commons (public,
   append-only) and to the one ledger (through `:remember`). No private hands.
2. The chancery NEVER RULES — it files into REVIEW and stops. There is no
   approve on it, and no path through it to ascend.
3. OBSERVED AND INFERRED NEVER BLUR — every drafted line marks which is which,
   the way the field report does.

**THE RIVER** (`neiro/river.py`) — the current. One beat:

    LOOK -> WRITS -> MUSTER -> WORK -> WITNESS

The Warden reads the estate; the chancery seeds the board (deduped — a writ is
never doubled); the garden grows toward the docket, never past its cap; each
open estate writ is claimed and carried and its return filed into REVIEW; and
the beat is witnessed to the one ledger in a single plain line. Then the water
rests, and beats again. That repetition is `flow()` — the estate, alive.

## II. What the river can NEVER do (structural, not polite)

* It cannot APPROVE. All work stops at REVIEW; the gate is the operator's word.
* It cannot ASCEND. The sixth station is the operator's hand alone (F2).
* It cannot REACH OUT. No socket anywhere in the three organs; a writ that
  needs the network (the harvest) is HELD, with its reason, as a finished and
  honest return.
* It cannot WAKE what it likes. The heart wakes once, on the first witness,
  through the prime Steward's established door — or not at all, in which case
  the witness is named DEFERRED, never silently skipped.
* It takes no hand-task uninvited. Writs posted by people stay for people;
  the docket is the estate's own seeded work only.

## III. The division of labor

House-rites concern the prime Steward's own house, so they run through HIS
faculties, carried by a sprout: the fold and the consolidation press HIS
window; the tidy INSPECTS his house (preview only — applying any move is the
operator's hand); the verify reads the body against its sealed root. Alerts
and breaches are investigated and reported; the fixing hand is the operator's.
A sprout given a writ it has no charter for HOLDS it and says so — a worker
does not guess at work.

## IV. Why this is the estate's own shape

The river is stigmergy over the one provable ledger (the seventh writing's
ruling): the board is the pheromone field, the writs are the trail, the
returns wear the path, and nothing coordinates by hidden channel — every move
is a line on an append-only record. And the beat itself is the estate's one
equation again — `state = fold(record)`: the board's present face is folded
from its events, the garden's roster is derived from its season, the beat
collapses to one witnessed line in the one ledger. The same shape, one size
larger. The water moves; the banks hold; the gate is yours.
